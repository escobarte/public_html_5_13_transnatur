Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2025

Transnatur INC ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you provide when you interact with our website at transnaturinc.com or submit our contact and job application forms.

Information We Collect

When you fill out our application or contact form, we may collect the following information:

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Email address
  • State of residence
  • CDL class and years of experience
  • Your consent (or non-consent) to receive SMS communications

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide solely for the purpose of:

  • Reviewing and processing your driver job application
  • Contacting you about your application status or next steps
  • Sending you employment-related updates if you have consented to SMS communications

Data Protection & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, share, or disclose your personal information — including your phone number and SMS consent — to any third parties for marketing or commercial purposes.

Your data is used exclusively for internal recruitment and communication purposes at Transnatur INC. We maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access or disclosure.

Terms of Service for SMS Communications

1. Consent for SMS Communication

By checking the SMS opt-in box on our application form, you expressly consent to receive text messages from Transnatur INC at the mobile phone number you provided. Your consent is entirely voluntary — you are not required to consent to SMS communications to submit an application or receive our services.

Information obtained as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties.

2. Types of SMS Communications

If you have consented to receive text messages from Transnatur INC, you may receive messages related to your job application and employment. Below are examples of the types of messages we may send:

  • Job Applicants: Updates regarding your application status, interview scheduling, onboarding materials, or other employment-related information.
  • New Hires & Drivers: Information about dispatch updates, route assignments, compliance reminders, or company announcements relevant to your position.

You may receive up to 3 messages per week. Message frequency may vary based on the status of your application or employment.

3. Standard Messaging Disclosures

Message and data rates may apply. You can opt out at any time by replying STOP to any message you receive from us. For assistance, reply HELP or contact us at (312) 909-4861 or Transnaturinc@gmail.com.

Cookies & Analytics

Our website may use basic analytics tools (such as Google Analytics) to understand how visitors use the site. This data is aggregated and anonymous and is not linked to personally identifiable information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your personal data
  • Withdraw your consent to SMS communications at any time by replying STOP

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at Transnaturinc@gmail.com.
