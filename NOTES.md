Updates on site from requester

I wanted to bring to your attention an important requirement for successfully completing the TCR process on your website. In order to proceed, it is mandatory to include opt-in language and a privacy policy on your website. 
For that reason, your TCR campaign was rejected. This is an explanation of the updates that you need to add to your website: 
1- Opt-In Language Requirement on Website for Phone Number Collection
It is essential to incorporate an opt-in and opt-out mechanism on the website if the intake form is utilized to collect phone numbers from customers. This entails adding an unchecked checkbox accompanied by a disclaimer stating, By checking this box, you agree to receive text messages from (COMPANY NAME) related to (add the type of text messages) at the phone number provided above. You will receive (1/3) messages per (day, week...). Message and data rates may apply. Also, you can get HELP at ( add number). You can reply STOP to opt-out of SMS messages. Please check or Visit [Privacy Policy URL] for privacy policy and [Terms URL] for Terms of Service”
Also, SMS Opt-in Box may not be prechecked or mandatory: Your website should include an opt-in checkbox that allows users to choose whether they wish to receive SMS notifications or not. This needs to be optional and should not force the customer to click on the checkbox to be able to submit the form.
2- Compliant Privacy Policy Requirement on Website
As the website collects customer information, it is imperative to have a comprehensive Privacy Policy section. This section should outline the company's practices regarding the use of customer data, emphasizing that personal information, such asphone numbers and SMS consent, will not be shared, sold, rented, or disclosed to any third parties for marketing purposes. 
 Terms and conditions of Service for SMS Communications must be included in the Privacy policy or be a stand-alone document, and include the following  (PLEASE DO NOT COPY AND PASTE IT)
           1- Consent for SMS Communication
      *Information obtained as part of the SMS consent process will not be shared with third parties. 
           2- Types of SMS Communications If you have consented to receive text messages from [Business Name], you may receive text messages related to [customer must provide example of messages being sent] Below are examples: Customers and Guests: Updates regarding your orders, deliveries, or other relevant information. Job Applicants: Information about your application status, onboarding materials, or other employment-related updates.
          3- Standard Messaging Disclosures Message and data rates may apply. You can opt-out at any time by texting "STOP." For assistance, text "HELP" or visit our [Privacy Policy URL] and [Terms of Service URL]
To facilitate a smooth TCR process, please ensure that your website includes both opt-in language and a privacy policy.
